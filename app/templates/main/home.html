Cybersecurity awareness should not be limited by the language a person speaks.

We work with Kenyan communities to develop, verify and validate cybersecurity terminology and awareness messages in the languages and dialects people understand best — so that a warning about a scam is understood, not just translated.

The challenge

A literal translation can sound unnatural, lose meaning, or even cause dangerous misunderstandings. Terms like phishing, malware and identity theft often have no familiar equivalent.

Our answer: community-developed, peer-verified terminology with linguistic and cybersecurity review.
