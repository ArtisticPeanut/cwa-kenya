About the project

Why we exist

Language barriers are an overlooked challenge in cybersecurity awareness. A person may receive a warning about a fraudulent message but fail to understand what makes it dangerous — or understand every word yet miss the intended meaning.

We build a trusted linguistic foundation for cybersecurity awareness in Kenya, developed with the people whose languages the platform supports.

How it works

  1. Choose the language, dialect or community you understand best.
  2. Contribute translations, definitions, usage notes and examples.
  3. Verify together — the community supports or disputes candidates.
  4. Review — translators and reviewers confirm cybersecurity accuracy before publication.

Our principles

Community first

Terminology is developed and validated by the communities that speak the language.

Meaning over words

We preserve the cybersecurity meaning, keeping messages culturally relevant and safe.

Transparent & auditable

Every approval is recorded with its revision history and review notes.

Fair recognition

Rewards reflect accuracy and constructive review, not just volume.

Open data

Verified translations can be exported as JSON or CSV, and served via a public API.

Privacy-conscious

Contributor records are minimised; public display is by display name only.

For developers & partners

Verified translations are available through a read-only API and as open downloads, so schools, businesses and awareness campaigns can reuse them.
